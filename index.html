Privacy Policy

BuildYourTrip · Last updated: July 4, 2026

Thank you for using BuildYourTrip. Your privacy matters, and this app is built to keep your information on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles and how.

The short version

BuildYourTrip does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. We have no servers and no user accounts. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information you enter

Trips, items, dates, prices, companions, receipt photos, and any text you paste are stored only on your device (and in your own iCloud or device backups, if you have those enabled). We have no access to this information.

On-device processing

When you paste a booking confirmation, the text is analyzed entirely on your device to fill in item details. On supported devices this may use Apple’s on-device intelligence. This text is not sent to us or to any server.

Apple services (maps & location lookup)

If you use the map feature, the addresses you entered are sent to Apple to convert them into map locations and to display the map. This is handled by Apple’s Maps services and is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy. We do not receive or store this information, and the app does not track your location.

Device permissions

The app asks for permission only when you use a related feature, and the resulting data stays on your device:

  • Camera & Photos — to attach receipt photos to items.
  • Calendar — to add a trip item to your calendar with a reminder. The app can add events but cannot read your calendar.

You can change or revoke these permissions anytime in the iOS Settings app.

What we do NOT do

  • No user accounts or sign-up.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No third-party data collection or SDKs that track you.

Children’s privacy

BuildYourTrip does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
